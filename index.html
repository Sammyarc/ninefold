How a project runs

Six weeks, no mystery.

  1. Week 1

    Listen

    Workshops with your team and customers. We leave with a one-page brief everyone signs.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3

    Design

    Two directions, then one. You see real pages, not mood boards.

  3. Weeks 4 to 5

    Build

    A staging link from day one. Weekly demos, no surprise invoices.

  4. Week 6

    Launch

    Go live, train your team and set the growth plan for the next quarter.
