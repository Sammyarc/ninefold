# Ninefold photography

Real photographs used as illustrative imagery for the fictional Ninefold studio. The people pictured are stock models: they are not staff, clients or endorsers, and the names, roles and quotes attached to them on the page are invented.

All photos are from Pexels under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). They were downloaded on November 23, 2026 through the Pexels CDN as cropped lossy WebP, and are self-hosted. Attribution is not required but is kept here. The photographers are credited on the linked Pexels pages.
